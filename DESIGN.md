# Tiny World Builder visual system
The user's pinned diorama direction governs this surface. A miniature garden on a sunlit worktable: tactile 3D geometry, generous cream negative space, quiet forest-green tools, and terracotta highlights.

The world is the primary working surface. A compact top bar frames a left object tray, a central island, a right atmosphere control, and a bottom camera toolbar. Selecting an object reveals a focused inspector. On phones, the tray becomes a collapsible bottom sheet.

DM Sans provides readable tool labels. Fraunces adds a small editorial voice to the world heading. Cream #f6f5ed, evergreen #284f3f, pale sage #e7eadc, muted olive #727b67, terracotta #bf6e4f. Corners are soft, shadows broad and low-contrast. Repeated actions share a 42px minimum touch target.

Signature moments: gently revealed island, procedural object settling animation, smoothly blended sunset with illuminated windows. All motion follows reduced-motion settings. No raster mockups: actual reusable 3D models also render library previews.
