# Tiny World Builder
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the explicit brief: React, TypeScript, Three.js, React Three Fiber; Vite for a client-only local-first application.
## Users
Visitors who want to relax by composing and customizing miniature floating islands on desktop and mobile.
## Product Purpose
Open directly into an attractive editable world. Make placement understandable within seconds, with reliable editing, local saving, and portable exports.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Three starter presets; at least fifteen reusable 3D models; constrained placement; object editing; undo/redo; day, sunset, night, rain; optional audio and reduced motion; validated versioned JSON; clean PNG postcard. No accounts or paid services. User explicitly requests implementation, local preview, production build, focused tests, and independent routine decisions.
## Brand Commitments
Cozy handcrafted miniature dioramas: sculpted earth, grass, sand, turquoise water, terracotta cottages, rounded vegetation, warm cream UI. Island dominates the viewport.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Labeled actions, keyboard alternatives, visible focus, comfortable touch targets, reduced motion, text explanations of errors and placement validity.
## Open Decisions
Assumption: prioritize evergreen WebGL2-capable browsers and device-local persistence. User has delegated routine design decisions and asked questions only for material blockers.
